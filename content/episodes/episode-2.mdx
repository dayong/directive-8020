## Episode 2: Best Laid Plans — Overview

18 hours after the crash. The Cassiopeia has crash-landed in the Bernell quadrant of Tau Ceti F. The ship is grounded — no fuel and a damaged FTL antenna. The crew's only option is to hold out until the Andromeda arrives (6 months away). They need to repair the antenna and deal with the mysterious Growth spreading through the ship.

> **Key Setup:** This episode establishes the crew's plan and relationships. Your choices here affect trust levels that carry through to Episodes 5 and 6.

---

## Scene 1: Situation Assessment

Eisele delivers the status report:
- Ship is grounded — cannot get back in the air
- FTL antenna damaged — no contact with Andromeda or Earth
- Life support operational but strained

**Stafford's three objectives:**
1. Find Sims (believed to be dangerous)
2. Repair the FTL antenna
3. Reestablish contact with the Andromeda

---

## Scene 2: The Growth Sample

The alien Growth has been confirmed as extraterrestrial — it arrived on the meteorite and spread to Carter and Sims' quarters.

### Key Choice: What to do with the Growth sample?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Run tests but don't put yourself at risk | Eisele gains data safely, crew trust maintained | ✓ Yes |
| Aggressively study the sample | More data but Eisele relationship risk | ✗ No |
| Destroy the sample immediately | Loses scientific data, Cooper relationship + | Situational |

**Stafford's order:** "Run whatever tests you need, but don't put yourself at risk."

## Scene 3: Stafford and Young — Private Conversation

Young and Stafford share a quiet moment looking out at Tau Ceti F.

### Key Choice: How do you respond to Stafford opening up?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Listen and offer support | Stafford relationship +, opens better Episode 5 options | ✓ Yes |
| Stay professional | Neutral, no relationship change | Neutral |
| Challenge his doubts | Stafford relationship — | ✗ No |

> **Stafford's confession:** "I lost a part of myself — the boots on the ground part that got me to where I am now. Up here, maybe I can find out."
> This moment is key to building the trust needed to unlock his Destiny later.
