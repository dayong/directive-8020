## Episode 8: The Finale — Overview

The final chapter reveals the truth behind **Directive 8020** — this is not the first time this has happened. **Cycle 13** is revealed. The crew must make it to the Caribdus probe and broadcast a warning to humanity.

> **Key Revelation:** The crew are clones. They are part of a corporate program (run by Corinth) that has sent clone crews to Tau Ceti F 13 times. Each cycle, the Growth destroys the crew. The real question is what Eisele does with the truth.

---

## The Caribdus Probe

With the ship dead and the Andromeda 6 months away, the crew has no air for the day. Then they discover **Caribdus** — one of 20 landing probes, each with a long-range FTL transmitter.

**Mission:** Reach the Caribdus probe and use its transmitter to contact the Andromeda.

---

## Cycle 13 Explained

> "Cycle 13 was a significant milestone for the program. For the first time, the life form intercepted the Cassiopeia outside orbit."

**What this means:**
- Corinth corporation has been sending clone crews to Tau Ceti F repeatedly
- Each cycle, the Growth (alien organism) destroys the crew
- The clones don't know they're clones
- Williams was a Corinth agent deployed to observe and report
- The "Directive 8020" refers to the neural control compound program — an attempt to weaponize/control the Growth

**The clone reveal:** Carter's wife and daughter are waiting for him at home — but Carter died on Cycle 13. The family doesn't know their "Carter" was a clone. This is why Carter's death hits differently on a second playthrough.

## Scene: The O Death Secrets

Five **O Death secrets** are hidden throughout the game. These reveal the full story of the Corinth clone program.

Finding all five unlocks the complete picture of what Directive 8020 really is.

**Locations:** Use Turning Points to revisit chapters and find the remaining secrets after your first playthrough.

## The Final Choice: Eisele's Destiny

This is the defining moment of the entire game.

### Key Choice: What does Eisele do with the truth?
| Option | Ending Unlocked | Description |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Expose the clone program (Eisele the Humanitarian)** | ✓ **TRUE ENDING** | Eisele broadcasts the truth to humanity. "Our fight for survival should never come at the cost of our humanity." Cycle ends. |
| Keep the information classified (Eisele the Scientist) | Science ending | Eisele prioritizes the scientific value of the Growth data. |

### How to Unlock "Eisele the Humanitarian" (True Ending)

Eisele must have the **"Humanitarian" Destiny** unlocked. This requires:

1. ✓ Eisele relationship maintained positively throughout all episodes
2. ✓ Real Eisele identified and saved in Episode 6
3. ✓ Eisele's scientific curiosity balanced with moral choices in Episodes 3-5
4. ✓ When prompted in Episode 8, choose **"Expose the truth"** over "Protect the mission"

**The True Ending Speech:**
> "It's a strange word, isn't it? Humanity. On one hand, it's a definition of us as a species. On the other, kindness, compassion, even love — attributes we've all but forgotten. Our fight for survival should never come at the cost of our humanity. We can no longer hide behind clones."
