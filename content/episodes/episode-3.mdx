## Overview — Cernan Takes the Wheel and Eisele's Path Forks

This is the first time you control Cernan, and the episode splits pretty evenly between him repairing the FTL antenna and Eisele getting way too curious about the Growth sample in the lab. I found this episode slow on my first run — it's less action-heavy than Ep4 or Ep6 — but the choices here are deceptively important, especially for Eisele's Destiny path.

How aggressively you push the Growth research directly feeds into whether she becomes the Humanitarian or the Scientist in Episode 8. Push too hard and you lock yourself onto the Scientist track without even realizing it until eight hours later. I made that mistake on my first playthrough and didn't understand why the true ending wasn't available at the finale.

> **The thing I missed:** The honest report you send to Earth about the Growth becomes part of the Corinth corporation's records. This ties directly into the Cycle 13 revelation in Episodes 7-8. If you omit details here, certain dialogue options later are closed off.

---

## Scene 1: Cernan's Introduction

You take control of **Cernan** for the first time. He is working on the FTL antenna repair with engineering support.

**Objective:** Reroute power and repair the antenna array using drones. ETA approximately one hour.

### Key Choice: How does Cernan approach the repair?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Methodical and careful | Antenna repaired cleanly, Cernan relationship + with Stafford | ✓ Yes |
| Rush the repair | Faster but risk of complications later | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 2: Private Conversation with Stafford

While working, Cernan and Stafford have a private moment.

Stafford reveals his doubts about leadership. This mirrors the Young/Stafford conversation from Episode 2.

### Key Choice: How does Cernan respond to Stafford?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Support and reassure Stafford | Stafford relationship +, supports Destiny unlock in Episode 5 | ✓ Yes |
| Defer professionally | Neutral, no change | Neutral |
| Challenge Stafford's doubts | Stafford relationship — | ✗ No |

> **Note:** Stafford says: "Delegation and dedication. That's what being a leader is about." Your response here contributes to whether Stafford's "The Father" Destiny unlocks in Episode 5.

---

## Scene 3: Eisele and the Growth Sample

While Cernan repairs the antenna, Eisele analyzes the Growth in the lab.

**Eisele's findings:**
- Organic and extraterrestrial in origin
- Rapidly replicating
- Almost certainly toxic
- Must have arrived via the meteorite

### Key Choice: How far does Eisele push the research?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Run standard tests, stay safe | Eisele relationship maintained, safer path | ✓ Yes |
| Push deeper into the sample | More data but Eisele takes risks | ✗ Risky |
| Quarantine immediately without testing | Loses data opportunity | Situational |

**Stafford's standing order:** "Don't put yourself at risk."

---

## Scene 4: The Growth Spreads

The Growth is confirmed to have spread beyond the initial quarantine zone.

> **Security Alert:** "Unidentifiable organic matter on science deck."

Eisele locks down the deck and seals the air vents to prevent further spread.

### Key Choice: Who helps Eisele contain the Growth?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Cernan assists Eisele | Cernan/Eisele relationship +, better coordination | ✓ Yes |
| Eisele handles it alone | Eisele relationship slight — (feels unsupported) | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 5: The Transmitter

Cernan completes the antenna repair. Earth contact is almost reestablished.

### Key Choice: Report everything to Earth honestly?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Full honest report about the Growth | Opens certain dialogue options in Episode 7-8 | ✓ Yes |
| Partial report — omit Growth details | Protects Corinth's interests | ✗ No |

> **Foreshadowing:** Whatever is reported here becomes part of the record that the Corinth corporation (Williams' employer) has access to. This ties into the Cycle 13 revelation later.

---

## Scene 6: What is Growing in the Reactor?

Images captured by Cooper show the meteorite lodged in the reactor array — the Growth's origin point on the ship.

**Theory confirmed:** Meteorite hit → Growth appears → Carter and Sims affected → Ship crash was intentional (alien defense mechanism).
