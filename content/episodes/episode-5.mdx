## Episode 5: Mr. Williams — Overview

The organism's mimicry ability becomes a crew-wide threat. Williams is revealed as a duplicate, and the crew must navigate a tense standoff at the biometric scanner. **This episode has the most missable decisions in the game.**

> **Critical Warning:** The sedative choice for Williams is easy to miss. If you don't actively choose to sedate Williams when given the opportunity, the option disappears permanently.

---

## Scene 1: Post-Episode 4 Debrief

The crew reconvenes. Eisele explains the organism's behavior:
- It replicates human cellular structure
- It uses human data (logs, memories) to perfect its mimicry  
- The mimics function like white blood cells — identify a threat, duplicate it, eliminate it

**Key Insight:** The organism crashed the ship deliberately. This is their territory, and the crew is trespassing.

---

## Scene 2: The Sedative — MISSABLE CHOICE

> **⚠ This is the most commonly missed choice in the game.**

When Eisele is in the lab analyzing the organism, she has the opportunity to **prepare a sedative for Williams**.

### Key Choice: Prepare sedative?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Prepare the sedative | Gives option to sedate Williams later, keeping him alive | ✓ Yes — required for "Sedate Williams" trophy |
| Skip / don't interact with sedative equipment | Williams cannot be sedated later, death more likely | ✗ Miss |

**How to trigger:** Interact with the lab equipment before leaving the science deck. The game does not clearly prompt this — actively explore the lab.

---

## Scene 3: Williams Revealed

Williams is exposed as a duplicate when scanned by the ship's biometric scanner.

**Scan result:** "Subject unidentifiable" — Williams is not human.

Before the crew can react, Williams begins to transform.

### Key Choice: Point Blank — Shoot or Stand Down?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Shoot | Williams duplicate is eliminated immediately | ✓ If you want to end the threat |
| Stand Down | Buys time, opens sedative option if prepared | ✓ If you prepared the sedative |
| Time Out | Minor delay, same outcomes as Stand Down | Neutral |

> **Note:** Many players report not seeing the shoot option clearly. It appears as a timed prompt during the transformation sequence.

---

## Scene 4: Scanner Standoff (Episode 6 Setup)

The crew retreats to Bridge Ops. Stafford orders everyone through the biometric scanner to verify who is human.

**Crew order through scanner:**
1. Young
2. Cernan  
3. Cooper
4. Eisele (last — this is intentional and suspicious)

**Important:** All 5 playable characters must pass through the scanner cleanly for the "All Crew Members Safely Passed Through" trophy.

---

## Scene 5: Mitchell and Anders

**⚠ Missable:** Mitchell and Anders can be killed in a fire event during this episode.

### Key Choice: Fire in the hydroponics bay
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Rescue both Mitchell and Anders | Both survive, trophy progress | ✓ Yes |
| Prioritize one over the other | One NPC dies | ✗ No |
| Ignore the fire | Both die | ✗ No |

---

## Stafford's Destiny Moment

Near the end of Episode 5, Stafford has a breakdown. Young rallies him by reciting his achievements.

**Correct response:** Encourage Stafford with "Never forget why you started."

**Result:** **Stafford's "The Father" Destiny is unlocked** — required for the best ending route.

> ✓ You should see "Destiny Unlocked" on screen if done correctly.
