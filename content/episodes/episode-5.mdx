## Overview

A mysterious figure known as **Mr. Williams** emerges aboard the Cassiopeia. This episode is a major turning point — choices here significantly narrow or expand your available ending paths.

> **Critical:** Your prior choices (especially weapon authorization in Episode 1) directly affect what options are available here.

---

## Scene 1: First Contact with Mr. Williams

The crew intercepts a transmission from someone identifying himself as "Mr. Williams." He claims to have information about the alien threat but demands something in return.

### Key Choice: Engage or Ignore

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Hear him out | Opens dialogue, gains intel about the alien | ✓ Yes |
| Ignore the transmission | Loses critical information, Episode 6 choices limited | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 2: The Deal

Mr. Williams proposes a deal: information about the alien's weakness in exchange for access to the ship's navigation system.

**Warning:** If you authorized weapons in Episode 1, you have a third option available here.

### Key Choice: The Negotiation

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Accept the deal | Gains alien weakness intel, but Mr. Williams gains navigation access | ⚠ Conditional |
| Refuse the deal | Mr. Williams becomes hostile, harder Episode 6 | ✗ No |
| **[Weapon Authorized]** Threaten him | Forces cooperation without giving up navigation. Best outcome | ✓ Yes (if available) |

---

## Scene 3: Stafford's Decision

Commander Stafford must decide how to handle the Mr. Williams situation. His decision affects the entire crew's safety.

### Key Choice: Containment Strategy

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Lock down the bridge | Stafford stays safe but isolated from crew | ✗ No |
| Evacuate non-essential personnel | Crew is safer but spread thin | ✓ Yes |
| Full assault on Mr. Williams' location | Dangerous, characters may die | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 4: Eisele's Discovery

Dr. Eisele analyzes data obtained from Mr. Williams and discovers something disturbing about the alien sample.

### Key Choice: Share or Withhold

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Share findings with the crew | Builds trust, crew makes better decisions | ✓ Yes |
| Keep findings confidential | Eisele Scientist path progress, but crew unity suffers | ✗ No (except for Scientist Destiny) |

---

## Scene 5: Cernan's Gamble

Cernan volunteers for a dangerous reconnaissance mission. His survival depends on both player QTEs and prior choices.

**QTE Sequence:** Three consecutive timed prompts. Missing any puts Cernan in critical danger.

### Key Choice: Reinforcements

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Send backup for Cernan | Cernan survives, but other areas are left unguarded | ✓ Yes |
| Trust Cernan to handle it alone | Cernan may die, higher risk | ✗ No |
