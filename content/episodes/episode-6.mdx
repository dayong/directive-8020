## Overview

This is the **most critical episode** for unlocking the true ending. A duplicate Eisele appears, the alien fully reveals itself, and the crew faces their greatest test. One wrong choice here can permanently close the path to "Eisele the Humanitarian."

> **This episode determines your ending.** Pay maximum attention — especially during the Eisele identification scene.

---

## Scene 1: Two Eisele's

The crew discovers two identical Dr. Eiseles, each claiming to be the real one. One is the genuine science officer; the other is an alien mimic.

**How to identify the real Eisele:**

- The **real Eisele** references specific data from Episode 3's sample analysis
- The **real Eisele** remembers the outcome of the Episode 2 Science Lab decision
- The **fake Eisele** makes small factual errors about past events
- The **fake Eisele** avoids direct eye contact during questioning

### Key Choice: Identify the Real Eisele

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Choose the Eisele who references Episode 3 data correctly | Real Eisele identified, true ending path remains open | ✓ Yes |
| Choose the wrong Eisele | The alien infiltrates the crew. True ending locked out. | ✗ No |
| Refuse to choose / stall | The alien escapes with both Eiseles. Both die. | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 2: The Takeover Begins

With the duplicate exposed (or not), the alien launches a full assault on the Cassiopeia's systems. Multiple characters are in danger simultaneously.

**QTE Gauntlet:** The longest QTE sequence in the game. Rotates between Young, Stafford, and Cernan. Stay focused.

### Key Choice: Priority Target

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Protect the bridge (Stafford's location) | Stafford survives, but other areas compromised | ✓ Yes |
| Protect the engine room (Cooper's location) | Cooper survives, but bridge is vulnerable | ✗ No |
| Split your attention | Weaker protection for all, characters may randomly die | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 3: Cooper's Stand

Cooper holds a critical chokepoint against the alien forces. This scene's outcome depends heavily on prior weapon authorization and backup decisions.

### Key Choice: Hold the Line

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Cooper holds position until reinforcements arrive | Cooper survives, alien advance is slowed | ✓ Yes |
| Cooper falls back to regroup | Cooper survives but alien advances faster | ⚠ Acceptable |
| Cooper makes a solo counterattack | Cooper likely dies, but buys maximum time | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 4: Eisele's Choice (True Ending Gate)

**This is the single most important choice in the game.** If the real Eisele was identified in Scene 1, she now faces a moral decision that determines the ending.

### Key Choice: The Sample

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Sacrifice the alien sample | Destroys the sample to save the crew. **Unlocks "Eisele the Humanitarian" true ending.** | ✓ Yes |
| Preserve the sample for science | Keeps the sample for research. Leads to "Eisele the Scientist" ending. | ✗ No (for true ending) |
| Use the sample as a weapon | Unpredictable results. May kill crew members. | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 5: Stafford's Final Command

With the alien threat contained (or not), Stafford must issue the final orders that set the course for the remaining episodes.

### Key Choice: Destination

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Set course for Earth | Homeward Bound ending paths open | ✓ Yes |
| Dock at nearest station | Docked ending paths open | ⚠ Alternative |
| Maintain current position | Alien threat persists, worse outcomes | ✗ No |
