## Episode 6: Hostile Takeover — Overview

The ship's systems are failing as the Growth spreads everywhere. The episode's climax features the most iconic moment in the game: **two Eiseles appear, and you must identify the real one.** This is the episode that determines whether you reach the best ending.

---

## Scene 1: Ship in Crisis

The Growth has spread to hydroponics and is overtaking the CO2 scrubbers. Power is failing across all decks.

Eisele's assessment: "The ship is dying. The Growth is spreading too fast."

**Stafford's order:** Eisele redirects power across all modules while the others secure the remaining crew.

---

## Scene 2: The Two Eiseles — THE MOST IMPORTANT MOMENT IN THE GAME

> **⚠ This is the critical decision that determines your ending.**

Two identical Eiseles appear. One is real, one is a mimic. **You must choose which one to trust.**

### How to Identify the Real Eisele

**Watch the animations carefully when Stafford points the gun at each one:**

| Eisele | Animation | Verdict |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| The one who came with the group (was present before this scene) | Movements look slightly unnatural, defensive reaction seems rehearsed | **FAKE — This is the mimic** |
| The newcomer (arrives separately during the confrontation) | Genuinely scared reaction, natural body language | **REAL — Trust this one** |

**The key tell:** The newcomer's fear response looks genuinely human. The one who was "with you" has subtly wrong animations — too controlled, not authentically panicked.

### Key Choice: Which Eisele do you trust?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Trust the newcomer (separate arrival) | Real Eisele survives, best ending path maintained | ✓ **Correct** |
| Trust the one who was with the group | Real Eisele dies, locked out of best ending | ✗ Wrong |

> **Tip from the community:** "When he points the gun at both of them, one looks like this [natural fear] and the other looks genuinely scared. The one with genuine fear is the real Eisele."

---

## Scene 3: After the Standoff

Once the real Eisele is confirmed, the crew must escape the failing ship sections.

**Power is completely gone.** Stafford guides the crew through backup systems.

### Key Choice: Stafford's Leadership Decision
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Stafford takes charge and focuses | Crew morale maintained, better Episode 7 outcomes | ✓ Yes |
| Stafford continues to doubt himself | Relationship penalties with crew | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 4: The Reactor Escape

The reactor is overloading. **Meltdown in 10 minutes.**

This is a timed sequence. Move quickly through each section:
- Meltdown timer: 10 minutes → 5 minutes → 3 minutes
- Fuel level must reach sufficient % for escape

### Critical Path Choice: Which direction outside?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Turn RIGHT (under the stalactites, stay outside) | Safe path, all crew escapes | ✓ Correct |
| Turn LEFT (into the cave system) | Point of no return — leads to Anders' death | ✗ Wrong |

> **⚠ Note:** The game does NOT clearly indicate which direction is correct. If you go left and enter the cave system, **you cannot go back.** Anders will die. This is one of the most frustrating forced deaths in the game.

---

## Anders' Death — Can It Be Prevented?

**Yes, but only if you turn RIGHT at the outdoor junction.**

Many players lose Anders here because:
1. The cave entrance looks like the intended path
2. The game forces you forward once you enter
3. There is no warning before the point of no return

**If you already lost Anders:** Use a Turning Point to go back to the outdoor junction and take the right path.
