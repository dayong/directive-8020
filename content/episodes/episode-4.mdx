## Overview — The Hunt and the Big Reveal

This is where Directive 8020 stops being a slow-burn mystery and becomes a straight-up horror movie. Young and Cooper go into the maintenance tunnels to hunt the creature that's been stalking the ship. Halfway through, you find Sims' body. She's been dead for a long time. The thing you've been chasing was never Sims — it's a mimic wearing her face.

I played this chapter with headphones on at 11 PM and the desk-hiding sequence actually made me jump. The stealth isn't mechanically deep, but the sound design and atmosphere carry it hard. Just follow Cooper's radio instructions and don't get creative. The one time I tried to investigate an alternate path, I got caught and had to replay ten minutes of stealth.

> **Key Revelation:** Sims has been dead the whole time. The organism can perfectly replicate human appearance. Every interaction from this point forward is suspect.

---

## Scene 1: Mission Briefing

Stafford authorizes use of the captain's revolver (6 rounds of hypkinetic soft point ammo). Eisele is confirmed as the authorized user.

**Plan:** Weld shut breach seals in three locations, sweep the tunnels, force the creature into a corner.

**Cooper** will seal bulkheads from above while Young navigates the tunnels.

---

## Scene 2: Maintenance Tunnels (Stealth Section)

You control **Young** navigating the maintenance tunnels. This is a stealth section — avoid detection.

### Navigation Tips
- Follow Cooper's directions carefully via radio
- Take the **window exit, not the door** when given the choice
- Hide behind the desk when instructed — the creature passes close by
- Seal each bulkhead in sequence: Shutter 1 → Shutter 2 → Shutter 3

### Key Choice: How do you proceed when you reach junction 13?
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Follow Cooper's instructions exactly | Safe navigation, both survive | ✓ Yes |
| Investigate alternate path | Risk of detection, collectibles possible | ✗ Risky |

---

## Scene 3: The Discovery — Sims is Dead

Upon opening Shutter 3, Young discovers **Sims' body — she has been dead for a long time.**

> **Important:** This confirms everything. The creature has been mimicking Sims the entire time. The real threat is the alien organism that can replicate human form.

After the discovery, the creature attacks. **QTE sequence follows immediately.**

### QTE Warning
A rapid button-tap sequence occurs here. Failure causes Young to lose an eye (permanent cosmetic change, does not affect survival if you complete the QTE partially).

---

## Scene 4: Reactor Confrontation

The creature is cornered near the reactor array.

### Key Choice: After the creature falls into the reactor
| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Confirm it's dead and report back | Crew reunites safely | ✓ Yes |
| Investigate further | Unnecessary risk | ✗ No |

**Outcome:** The creature falls into the reactor funnel. Young reports back to the crew.
