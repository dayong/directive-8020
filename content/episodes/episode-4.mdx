## Overview

The alien threat spreads through the Cassiopeia. The crew must decide who to trust as paranoia takes hold. Key decisions here affect which ending paths remain available, and **Cooper** faces their first major life-or-death scenario.

---

## Scene 1: The Search Begins

The episode opens with a briefing. Stafford orders a systematic search of the ship, but tensions are high after the events of Episode 3.

> **Tip:** Pay close attention to dialogue options here. Character relationships from Episodes 1-3 carry forward and affect available choices.

### Key Choice: Search Strategy

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Split into pairs | Faster coverage, but characters are isolated | ✗ No |
| Stay together | Slower but safer — characters can support each other | ✓ Yes |

## Scene 2: Cooper's Discovery

While searching the lower decks, **Cooper finds something alarming** in the cargo hold.

**QTE Warning:** A series of rapid button-tap QTEs follows. These are not scripted-fail — you must succeed to keep Cooper alive.

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |

## Scene 3: Trust Tested

The alien entity begins manipulating the crew through ship systems. False alarms and fake distress calls create chaos.

**Collectible:** After the alarm sequence, check the medical bay for a hidden recording.

### Key Choice: Who Do You Trust?

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |

| Trust Stafford's judgment | Maintains command structure, Stafford relationship + | ✓ Yes |

## Scene 4: Confrontation

The episode climax involves a direct confrontation with the alien threat. Cooper's survival is on the line.

**Critical QTE:** A timed aiming sequence — aim for the **ventilation pipe** (not the creature itself) to create a distraction.

### Key Choice: Save or Sacrifice

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Create distraction, everyone escapes | All characters survive this scene | ✓ Yes |
| Attack the creature directly | Cooper may die, but the threat is temporarily neutralized | ✗ No |
