## Overview

The opening episode introduces you to the Cassiopeia crew and the hull breach that sets the story in motion. You primarily control **Brianna Young**.

> **Important:** Carter and Simms will die during scripted story events in this episode. Their deaths **cannot be prevented** regardless of your choices. Do not waste time trying to save them.

---

## Scene 1: Hull Breach

After the opening cinematic, you gain control of Young.

**Explore the area** before advancing — the first **Simms Recording** collectible is up the stairs to the left of the objective door.

### Key Choice: Initial Response

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Secure the breach | Crew relationship +, slower progress | ✓ Yes |
| Evacuate immediately | Faster but loses trust | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 2: Medical Complex

The power is out and you need to restore it to access Medical.

**Tutorial:** Use your scanner to trace power lines. Follow the trail to the power device in the wall — interact and redirect flow to the vent.

**QTE Warning:** A button-tap QTE follows immediately after — this QTE **always fails** by design. Prepare to run.

### Key Choice: Secured / Checkmate

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Honor | Stafford relationship +, better Episode 4 options | ✓ Yes |
| Implore | No significant benefit | ✗ No |

After choosing Honor:

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Consider | Maintains crew trust | ✓ Yes |
| Doubt | Minor relationship penalty | ✗ No |

---

## Scene 3: Crew Quarters (Turning Point)

Your first **Turning Point** appears here. This is a checkpoint you can return to later if you want to explore alternate paths.

**Collectible:** Explore the Crew Quarters thoroughly before advancing — there are 2 secrets in this area.

### Key Choice: Authorize Weapon

| Option | Consequence | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Authorize use of weapon | Opens combat option later | ✓ Yes (for best ending) |
| Refuse | Limits options in Episode 5 | ✗ No |

---

## Scripted Deaths (Cannot Be Prevented)

**Carter** dies during a scripted event at the end of Episode 1. There is no choice or QTE that prevents this.

**Simms** becomes corrupted in a scripted event. Her death is also unavoidable.

This is intentional — the game has 5 playable characters but 6 total, because the co-op mode supports 5 players. Carter and Simms serve as the narrative catalyst.

- [ ] Chose **Honor** in Secured/Checkmate choice
- [ ] Chose **Consider** in follow-up choice
- [ ] Authorized weapon use
- [ ] Completed all QTEs (except the scripted-fail one)
